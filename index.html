Yay List（以下稱「本 App」）由個人開發者發布，不隸屬於任何公司。我們重視你的隱私，本政策說明本 App 如何處理你的資料。

一句話總結

你的願望清單、照片、紀錄只存在你的裝置和你自己的 iCloud 帳號裡。我們沒有伺服器，看不到你的任何資料，也不會拿去打廣告。

我們不會做的事

  • 不要求註冊或登入帳號
  • 不蒐集姓名、Email、電話等個人識別資訊
  • 不使用任何廣告追蹤或分析工具（SDK）
  • 不會把你的資料賣給或分享給第三方
  • 不會將你的資料上傳到我們自己的伺服器——因為我們根本沒有伺服器

你的資料存在哪裡

資料類型儲存位置說明
願望清單、準備事項、Yay 時刻紀錄、旅程日誌你的裝置本機 + 你自己的 iCloud 私人資料庫透過 Apple CloudKit 技術同步，只在你自己的裝置之間同步（例如你的 iPhone 和 iPad），我們無法讀取
Yay 時刻與旅程紀錄的照片原始照片存在你新增照片的那台裝置；壓縮後的版本（最長邊約 1600 像素）會同步到你自己的 iCloud讓你換手機、或共享清單的成員也看得到；原始檔不會同步
品鑑紀錄的照片僅儲存在你的裝置本機不會透過 iCloud 同步
足跡（你去過的國家）你的裝置本機 + 你自己的 iCloud 私人資料庫只記錄國家、年月、每個月一個代表座標、照片張數；不包含照片本身。座標是該月第一張帶位置的照片的位置，而且只存到小數點後 2 位（精度約 1 公里，不是精確位置）；同樣只在你自己的 iCloud 裡，我們無法讀取
App 內設定（暱稱、語言、主題等）你的裝置本機不會離開你的裝置

你可以隨時在「我」→「資料與隱私」→「匯出我的資料」，把所有紀錄匯出成 Markdown 與圖片檔，存到你自己的「檔案」App 或 iCloud Drive。這份匯出檔不需要本 App 也能開啟閱讀。

需要你授權的系統權限

權限用途何時使用
照片（選擇）選擇照片加入 Yay 時刻、旅程紀錄或品鑑紀錄只在你主動點選「選擇照片」時才會詢問，只能讀到你挑選的那幾張
照片（讀取相簿）足跡地圖：讀取照片的「拍攝地點與日期」，整理出你去過哪些國家只在你按下「從相簿整理足跡」或點進國家頁時才會詢問；只讀位置與日期，不上傳、不複製照片；全部在你的手機上處理，國家判定用 App 內建的國界資料、不連網。你可以選「有限照片存取」，只讓 App 看到你挑選的照片
通知願望到期前提醒、準備事項提醒、每月回顧、久未開啟提醒、進度里程碑都是你手機上的本機通知，不經過任何伺服器；每一種都能在「我」分頁個別關閉

關於飛機上的照片：足跡會自動略過明顯在高空或高速移動中拍的照片，但機上拍照常常沒有高度資料，所以有可能漏掉。App 會在國家清單提示「只有 1～3 張照片」，你可以點進去看照片，不是你去的就能移除。

共享清單（你主動邀請的人）

你可以把某張清單分享給家人朋友一起計劃。這是你主動選擇才會發生的事，沒有分享就不會有任何人看到你的內容。

  • 誰看得到：你分享出去的那一張清單，裡面的願望、準備事項、費用紀錄、旅行資訊、品鑑紀錄、旅程日誌、Yay 時刻，以及（壓縮後的）照片，加入的成員都看得到、也都能編輯。品鑑照片與原始照片不會被分享
  • 成員名單：成員會看到彼此在名冊上的名字（你自己取的暱稱），以及誰新增了什麼
  • 怎麼加入：邀請以連結或 QR code 進行。拿到連結的人就能加入，所以請只傳給你信任的人，不要公開張貼
  • 資料經過哪裡：分享透過 Apple 的 iCloud（CloudKit）進行，內容只在你與受邀成員的 iCloud 之間流動，不經過我們，我們同樣看不到
  • 停止分享：你可以隨時在「一起計劃」頁面停止分享。已經同步到其他成員裝置上的內容，我們無法替你從對方的裝置收回

選用功能：發布到你自己的 WordPress 網站

本 App 提供「發布到 WordPress」的選用功能，讓你可以把 Yay 時刻或旅程紀錄發布到你自己架設、你自己擁有的 WordPress 網站。

  • 這個功能完全由你主動觸發，不使用就不會有任何資料傳出
  • 你需要自行輸入你的網站網址與「應用程式密碼」（WordPress 官方提供的登入方式，不是你的帳號主密碼）
  • 這組密碼加密儲存在你裝置的 Keychain（iOS 系統層級的加密保險箱），我們無法讀取，也不會傳送到我們或任何第三方
  • 按下發布後，內容會直接從你的裝置傳送到你自己的網站，不會經過我們

兒童隱私

本 App 不是設計給兒童使用，也不會刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料。

政策異動

若本 App 未來的資料處理方式有變（例如新增帳號登入、改變資料儲存位置，或新增其他會把資料傳給他人的功能），我們會更新這份政策並標註新的更新日期。

聯絡我們

若你對這份隱私權政策有任何問題，請透過 App Store 上的開發者聯絡方式與我們聯繫。

English Version

Last Updated: 2026-10-16

Yay List (the "App") is published by an independent developer, not affiliated with any company. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

In short

Your wishes, photos, and records live only on your device and in your own iCloud account. We don't run a server, we can't see your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

What we don't do

  • No account registration or sign-in required
  • We don't collect your name, email, phone number, or other personal identifiers
  • We don't use any advertising or analytics tracking SDKs
  • We never sell or share your data with third parties
  • We never upload your data to a server we control — because we don't operate one

Where your data lives

DataStorage locationNotes
Wishes, preparation steps, Yay moments, journal entriesYour device + your own private iCloud databaseSynced via Apple's CloudKit technology, only between your own devices (e.g. your iPhone and iPad). We cannot read it.
Photos in Yay moments and journal entriesThe original stays on the device where you added it; a compressed version (about 1600 px on the long side) syncs to your own iCloudSo the photo shows up on your other devices and for members of a shared list; the original file is not synced
Photos in tasting logsStored locally on your device onlyNot synced via iCloud
Footprints (countries you've visited)Your device + your own private iCloud databaseRecords only the country, the month, one representative coordinate per month, and the photo count — never the photos themselves. The coordinate is the location of the first geotagged photo that month, stored only to two decimal places (about 1 km — not an exact position); it lives only in your own iCloud and we cannot read it
App settings (nickname, language, theme, etc.)Local to your deviceNever leaves your device

You can export all your records at any time via Profile → Data & Privacy → Export My Data, producing Markdown files and photos you can save to the Files app or your own iCloud Drive. The export is readable without the App.

System permissions we ask for

PermissionPurposeWhen used
Photos (picker)Choose photos to attach to a Yay moment, journal entry, or tasting logOnly requested when you tap "Choose Photos"; the app can only read the photos you pick
Photos (library access)Footprints: read the location and date of your photos to work out which countries you've visitedOnly requested when you tap "Scan photo library" or open a country page; only location and date are read — photos are never uploaded or copied; everything happens on your phone, and country matching uses border data bundled in the app (no network). You can choose "Limited Access" so the app sees only photos you select
NotificationsTarget-date reminders, preparation reminders, monthly review, a nudge if you haven't opened the app, progress milestonesAll are local notifications on your phone, never through a server; each type can be turned off individually in the Profile tab

About photos taken on a plane: Footprints automatically skips photos that were clearly taken at altitude or at high speed, but photos taken in flight often carry no altitude data, so some may slip through. The app flags countries with only 1–3 photos in the country list so you can open them, check the photos, and remove a country that isn't yours.

Shared lists (people you choose to invite)

You can share a list with family or friends to plan together. This only happens if you choose to — nothing is visible to anyone until you share.

  • Who can see it: everything in the list you share — wishes, preparation steps, expenses, travel info, tasting logs, journal entries, Yay moments, and (compressed) photos — is visible and editable by members who join. Tasting photos and original photos are not shared
  • Member list: members see each other's names on the member list (the nickname you set) and who added what
  • How people join: by link or QR code. Anyone who has the link can join, so send it only to people you trust and never post it publicly
  • Where the data goes: sharing works through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit); content flows only between your iCloud and your invited members' iCloud accounts — never through us, and we cannot see it either
  • Stopping: you can stop sharing at any time from the Plan Together screen. Content that has already synced to other members' devices can't be recalled by us

Optional feature: Publish to your own WordPress site

The App offers an optional "Publish to WordPress" feature that lets you publish a Yay moment or journal entry to a WordPress site you own and operate.

  • This only happens when you explicitly trigger it — nothing is sent if you don't use it
  • You provide your own site URL and a WordPress "Application Password" (a WordPress-provided login method, not your main account password)
  • This credential is stored encrypted in your device's Keychain (iOS's system-level secure storage) and is never readable by us or transmitted to any third party
  • When you tap publish, content goes directly from your device to your own site — it never passes through us

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If how the App handles data changes in the future (e.g. adding account sign-in, changing where data is stored, or adding other features that send data to other people), we will update this policy and note the new date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the developer contact information on the App Store.
